What NutriScan looks at

Certain nutrient deficiencies leave visible, well-documented signs on the body. NutriScan uses three specialized AI models — one per body part — trained to recognize those signs from a simple photo.

👁️

Eye

Screens for signs associated with Vitamin A and Iron deficiency, such as changes in the sclera and surrounding tissue.

💅

Nail

Checks nail color, texture, and shape for patterns linked to Iron and Zinc deficiency — pallor, spooning, and leukonychia.

👅

Tongue

Analyzes tongue color and surface texture for signs linked to Vitamin B deficiency, including redness and fissuring.
